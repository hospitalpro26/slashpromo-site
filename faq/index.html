Help & FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Clear answers about healthcare campaigns, featured profiles, advertising and how SlashPromo works.

Is SlashPromo a doctor-booking platform?

No. SlashPromo is a promotional showcase. For current bookings, schedules and provider details, follow the linked official website or Slashcure profile.

Which cities are covered?

SlashPromo welcomes healthcare promotions from any city in Pakistan. Existing city pages are examples of published spotlights, not a restriction on where providers may advertise.

Can a hospital or clinic advertise here?

Yes. Healthcare organizations can send an inquiry with approved material and their official links. Availability and pricing are discussed separately.

Are all featured profiles paid advertisements?

No. Initial cards include editorial examples based on published information. Paid placements must be labelled Sponsored or Advertisement.

Can a featured profile be corrected or removed?

Yes. Submit a correction or removal request by email with the relevant URL and supporting information.

Does SlashPromo verify clinical outcomes or rank providers?

No. A promotional appearance is not a medical endorsement or proof of superiority. Visitors should evaluate qualifications, services and current information independently.

Does SlashPromo collect payment online?

Not in this launch version. It currently accepts inquiries by email, not payments or online booking requests.
