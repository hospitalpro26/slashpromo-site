Privacy Policy

How SlashPromo handles inquiries and website information.

Updated: 9 November 2026 · Applies to the SlashPromo website.

Information you provide

SlashPromo accepts business and visitor inquiries through email or an external messaging service and provides moderated Questions & Answers on city pages and local doctor, hospital, specialty and health-topic pages in supported cities. When you contact us, we may receive your name, email address, organization, phone number and any information you choose to send. Please do not email private patient records or sensitive medical information.

Questions & Answers on city and local healthcare pages

When you submit a question through a supported Q&A section, we record the source page so it can appear in the appropriate moderated discussion. We collect your name and city (required), and may collect your WhatsApp number and suggestions for improvements (optional). The form now requires an explicit agreement for your submitted name to appear publicly alongside your question if that question is approved. Names collected earlier under a private-name notice remain private unless we obtain separate permission. Your entered city, WhatsApp number, and suggestions remain private and available only to authorized SlashPromo administrators for handling inquiries and improving the service. If you provide a WhatsApp number and separately consent to contact, our team may use it to contact you about that question. We never display your entered city, WhatsApp number, or suggestions in the public Questions & Answers section.

The question itself is held for moderation. Only if approved, the question text, the name you consented to publish, and our editorial answer may be published only on the relevant city or Sukkur healthcare page. Questions from different pages are kept in separate public discussion threads; a moderator can identify their page of origin. Please do not include private contact information, patient names, identifiable symptoms, medical reports or other sensitive details in the question or suggestion. Submissions are not a confidential medical consultation or an emergency service.

We use Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent automated submissions and maintain a keyed, pseudonymous IP digest for submission limits. Cloudflare may process IP and security information for verification. Submitted details are retained for handling questions and feedback and may be deleted when no longer required. You may request correction or removal by contacting info@slashcure.com.

How information is used

We use inquiry information to answer questions, assess proposed promotions, resolve corrections and administer website communications. We do not publish private contact information without authorization.

WhatsApp-first contact and existing private enquiries

The floating WhatsApp form on SlashPromo public pages asks only for your name, city or area, and message. It does not ask for an email address or phone number. These details stay in your browser while you compose. Selecting Continue to WhatsApp opens a WhatsApp draft containing the details you choose to include plus the source-page title and URL. The form no longer sends a private enquiry to the SlashPromo database, and it does not send an email or WhatsApp message automatically. You must press Send within WhatsApp to deliver the message. WhatsApp/Meta handles messages under its own terms and privacy practices. You can alternatively contact us by email using the Contact page. Do not include patient records or sensitive health information.

Previously submitted private enquiries: Earlier enquiries saved with your explicit permission and Turnstile verification remain held in the access-controlled Cloudflare D1 Enquiry Inbox, along with any contact details you chose to provide. They are not published in public directories or included in aggregate website analytics. Authorised staff can review these records, follow up and mark their statuses. Closed enquiries are automatically removed after 180 days under the existing retention rules, or you can request access, correction or deletion via info@slashcure.com. The prior short-lived anti-abuse rate-limit digest expires after up to two days; raw IPs are not stored in the enquiry record.

Private staff replies: Authorised administrators may save a reply draft or prepare a WhatsApp reply for an existing consented enquiry. Opening WhatsApp does not confirm the reply was actually sent. Staff drafts and prepared replies remain private, are never added to public Q&A or analytics and follow the related enquiry retention policy.

Private operational Control Center

Authorised SlashPromo staff can access a private Control Center that summarises saved Q&A review statuses, consented enquiry follow-up statuses, overdue queues and aggregate site traffic. Its reporting API is secured with the same private administrator key as the Q&A and Enquiry Inbox APIs. The Control Center lists limited source-page and record metadata but does not return visitor names, phone numbers, home addresses or message bodies. A dashboard alert means a record is pending action; it is not a push, SMS or email notification. Generic quick-reply templates are processed in the staff browser, edited before use and never automatically sent to visitors.

Encrypted backups and access-controlled exports

Authorised administrators may export selected operational records only through a restricted backup interface that requires both a verified Cloudflare Access identity and a separate administrator key. Exports may include private names, phone numbers, enquiries and reply histories. The browser encrypts the supplemental logical archive locally before download using a separately chosen passphrase. A restore is not performed through the website. Administrators are responsible for keeping encrypted copies secure, limiting access and ensuring their handling aligns with the retention policies for personal information. We do not expose decrypted backups or individual visitor data in public analytics, public pages or GitHub. The primary database has Cloudflare-managed recovery history subject to the account plan and database storage capabilities.

Anonymous site analytics and approximate visitor geography

SlashPromo uses first-party, cookie-free aggregate counters to understand which public pages are viewed and how often visitors follow Slashcure links, open the WhatsApp enquiry form, continue to WhatsApp, or submit moderated Q&A questions. Basic analytics stores only the UTC date, public source-page path, derived directory-page city, event type and count. For page views from the date this feature is enabled, we also aggregate Cloudflare's approximate IP-based visitor country and city together with UTC date, directory-page city and page-view count. These estimates can be wrong, especially for visitors using VPNs or mobile networks, and do not reveal an exact address or GPS location. Visitor-country and visitor-city counts are displayed only to authenticated administrators, not to the public.

We do not store raw visitor IP addresses, personal identifiers, names, device IDs, GPS coordinates, WhatsApp or mobile numbers, full referrers, search queries or medical messages in the analytics tables. The WhatsApp form prepares its outgoing message in the visitor's browser; its content is not sent to the analytics API. Counts measure page loads and actions, not unique individuals or confirmed messages, appointments or bookings. Historical geography cannot be reconstructed for visits before this update. We do not use behavioural advertising tracking cookies.

Optional nearby doctor suggestions

After you navigate to two different public pages within an hour (including separate browser tabs), SlashPromo may show a small, dismissible invitation to use your location for more relevant nearby doctor and hospital listings. This is optional: the website continues to work if you choose Not now or deny access. We never trigger the browser's location permission request without you clicking Use my location. If you agree, your browser provides coordinates to our on-page code to find an approximate nearest listed city. Your exact coordinates are neither sent to SlashPromo servers nor saved in website analytics or browser storage; only the resulting directory-city name is kept locally in your browser for up to 30 days to prioritize matching profiles. The site also remembers a one-hour page counter and the last public page path in this browser (not a full browsing history). A displayed invitation is snoozed for 24 hours, and an explicitly dismissed invitation for seven days. You can remove the saved city preference using the control beside directory results, or clear this site's storage in your browser. Navigation to a city search URL may include a city name in the address. Browser location access can also be changed at any time through your browser's site permissions.

Hosting and technical data

The website is delivered through Cloudflare Pages. The hosting and security infrastructure may process IP addresses, device details and request logs to provide the site, prevent abuse and maintain service reliability. We count aggregate outbound clicks on sponsored campaign links without retaining individual visitor identifiers for these counts. We do not currently add behavioral advertising or visitor profiling scripts. Approved campaign images may be loaded from external image providers, subject to their own privacy practices.

Doctor directory searches

The doctor finder filters SlashPromo's own public directory within your browser. Search terms are not sent to Slashcure or another search service, and the directory search does not create an individual search-history account. Opening a published external profile later is a separate action governed by that provider's privacy terms.

Third-party websites

Links to Slashcure and other external sites are governed by their respective privacy policies. We are not responsible for their information practices.

Retention, security and requests

We aim to keep inquiry information only as long as reasonably necessary for communication, legitimate operational needs and applicable obligations. Email and third-party messaging carry inherent risks. You can request access, correction or deletion of information you provided by contacting info@slashcure.com, subject to applicable requirements.

Changes

We may update this policy when new services, analytics, submission forms or other features are introduced. Any material changes will be reflected on this page.
