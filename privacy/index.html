Privacy Policy

How SlashPromo handles inquiries and website information.

Updated: 8 November 2026 · Applies to the SlashPromo website.

Information you provide

SlashPromo accepts business and visitor inquiries through email or an external messaging service and provides moderated Questions & Answers on city pages and local doctor, hospital, specialty and health-topic pages in supported cities. When you contact us, we may receive your name, email address, organization, phone number and any information you choose to send. Please do not email private patient records or sensitive medical information.

Questions & Answers on city and local healthcare pages

When you submit a question through a supported Q&A section, we record the source page so it can appear in the appropriate moderated discussion. We collect your name and city (required), and may collect your WhatsApp number and suggestions for improvements (optional). The form now requires an explicit agreement for your submitted name to appear publicly alongside your question if that question is approved. Names collected earlier under a private-name notice remain private unless we obtain separate permission. Your entered city, WhatsApp number, and suggestions remain private and available only to authorized SlashPromo administrators for handling inquiries and improving the service. If you provide a WhatsApp number and separately consent to contact, our team may use it to contact you about that question. We never display your entered city, WhatsApp number, or suggestions in the public Questions & Answers section.

The question itself is held for moderation. Only if approved, the question text, the name you consented to publish, and our editorial answer may be published only on the relevant city or Sukkur healthcare page. Questions from different pages are kept in separate public discussion threads; a moderator can identify their page of origin. Please do not include private contact information, patient names, identifiable symptoms, medical reports or other sensitive details in the question or suggestion. Submissions are not a confidential medical consultation or an emergency service.

We use Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent automated submissions and maintain a keyed, pseudonymous IP digest for submission limits. Cloudflare may process IP and security information for verification. Submitted details are retained for handling questions and feedback and may be deleted when no longer required. You may request correction or removal by contacting info@slashcure.com.

How information is used

We use inquiry information to answer questions, assess proposed promotions, resolve corrections and administer website communications. We do not publish private contact information without authorization.

Consented WhatsApp enquiries and private inbox

When you choose to save an enquiry in the floating WhatsApp form, we collect your name, city or area, enquiry text and optional WhatsApp contact number, together with the public SlashPromo page title, page path and time of submission. We save these details in our existing private Cloudflare D1 database only after you explicitly consent and complete Cloudflare Turnstile verification. We use them to respond to your enquiry and manage follow-up; no saved enquiry is published publicly or included in site analytics. Do not enter full home addresses, patient identifiers, medical reports or highly sensitive health information. Administrative staff can mark the enquiry New, Contacted, Resolved or Closed and add private follow-up notes. Closed enquiries are automatically removed after 180 days; otherwise you can request access, correction or deletion using info@slashcure.com. For anti-abuse limits, a short-lived keyed IP digest is kept separately for up to two days; the raw IP is not stored in the enquiry record.

After a successful private save, the site offers a link that prepares a WhatsApp message with your submitted details and source page. Opening that link is not evidence you actually sent the WhatsApp message. WhatsApp has its own privacy practices. If you prefer not to store an enquiry, you can select the direct WhatsApp contact link without using the save form.

Private staff replies: When our team replies to a consented enquiry, an authorised administrator may privately save a response draft or prepare a WhatsApp reply. These replies are retained in the Enquiry Inbox alongside the original enquiry. Preparing a WhatsApp reply does not send it. Staff may explicitly record that they pressed Send, but this is not a delivery or read confirmation. Draft and prepared replies remain private, are never added to public Q&A or analytics, and are deleted with the related closed enquiry under the 180-day closed-record retention policy. Visitors may request correction or deletion via info@slashcure.com.

Anonymous site analytics

SlashPromo uses first-party, cookie-free aggregate event counters to understand which pages are viewed and how often people click links to Slashcure, open the WhatsApp enquiry form, continue to WhatsApp, or successfully submit a moderated Q&A question. The analytics database stores only the UTC date, public source-page path, derived page city, event type and count. We do not store visitor IP addresses, identifiers, names, device IDs, WhatsApp numbers, full referrers, queries or medical messages in analytics. The WhatsApp form prepares its message in the visitor’s browser and its content is not sent to the analytics API. These metrics measure actions, not unique people or confirmed WhatsApp messages and bookings. Reports are restricted to authorized administrators; we do not use behavioural advertising tracking cookies.

Hosting and technical data

The website is delivered through Cloudflare Pages. The hosting and security infrastructure may process IP addresses, device details and request logs to provide the site, prevent abuse and maintain service reliability. We count aggregate outbound clicks on sponsored campaign links without retaining individual visitor identifiers for these counts. We do not currently add behavioral advertising or visitor profiling scripts. Approved campaign images may be loaded from external image providers, subject to their own privacy practices.

Doctor directory searches

The doctor finder filters SlashPromo's own public directory within your browser. Search terms are not sent to Slashcure or another search service, and the directory search does not create an individual search-history account. Opening a published external profile later is a separate action governed by that provider's privacy terms.

Third-party websites

Links to Slashcure and other external sites are governed by their respective privacy policies. We are not responsible for their information practices.

Retention, security and requests

We aim to keep inquiry information only as long as reasonably necessary for communication, legitimate operational needs and applicable obligations. Email and third-party messaging carry inherent risks. You can request access, correction or deletion of information you provided by contacting info@slashcure.com, subject to applicable requirements.

Changes

We may update this policy when new services, analytics, submission forms or other features are introduced. Any material changes will be reflected on this page.
