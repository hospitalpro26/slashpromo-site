Advertising Policy

How sponsored healthcare promotions are reviewed and labelled.

Effective: 5 November 2026 · Applies to the SlashPromo website.

Paid placements

Paid promotions must be clearly identified as “Sponsored” or “Advertisement.” Editorial features are not presented as paid endorsements. Advertising does not buy influence over clinical facts or imply provider superiority.

Required substantiation

Healthcare advertisers must provide accurate organization names, locations, services and appropriate authorizations for photographs, logos and professional claims. Testimonials, comparative rankings, outcomes, discounts and fees require additional support and review.

Prohibited or restricted promotions

We do not accept deceptive health claims, guaranteed cure claims, impersonation, unauthorized patient images, or promotion of unlawful or unsafe healthcare services. Additional categories may be restricted at our discretion to protect audiences and comply with applicable requirements.

Changes, complaints and removal

We may reject, revise, pause or remove a campaign when information is inaccurate, authorization is unclear, or it could mislead patients. Send concerns with URLs and supporting details to info@slashcure.com.

Advertising terms

Campaign pricing, placement duration, creative rights and cancellation terms are agreed directly with each advertiser. No self-service payments are offered on this launch website.
