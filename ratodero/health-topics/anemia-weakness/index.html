Anemia, Fatigue and Weakness

Fatigue and weakness have many causes, including anemia, thyroid disorders, chronic illness, sleep problems and medication effects.

Symptoms and signs

Anemia may cause tiredness, pallor, dizziness, exertional breathlessness or palpitations, but symptoms alone cannot confirm low hemoglobin.

Diagnosis and medical follow-up

A clinician may advise a complete blood count and other targeted investigations to identify the cause. Iron or vitamin supplements should fit the diagnosis rather than be assumed necessary.

When to seek urgent care

Fainting, chest pain, severe breathlessness or heavy ongoing bleeding requires urgent assessment.
