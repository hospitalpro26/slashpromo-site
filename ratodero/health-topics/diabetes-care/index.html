Diabetes Symptoms and Care in Ratodero

Understanding diabetes (شوگر): common signs, monitoring and when to get medical help.

Common symptoms

Frequent urination, excessive thirst, unexplained weight loss, tiredness and blurred vision may occur with high blood glucose. Some people have no symptoms, so testing matters for people at risk.

What follow-up may involve

A clinician may review glucose readings, HbA1c, blood pressure, kidney function, medicines and foot health based on individual needs. Do not alter prescribed treatment without medical advice.

Seek urgent care

Vomiting with marked dehydration, deep or rapid breathing, confusion or reduced consciousness may signal a medical emergency.
