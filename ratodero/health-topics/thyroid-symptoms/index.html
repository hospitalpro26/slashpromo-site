Thyroid Symptoms and Medical Review in Ratodero

What an underactive or overactive thyroid may feel like — and how doctors investigate it.

Underactive thyroid (hypothyroidism)

Possible symptoms include fatigue, constipation, feeling cold, dry skin and unexplained weight gain. Symptoms alone cannot confirm a thyroid diagnosis.

Overactive thyroid (hyperthyroidism)

Palpitations, tremor, heat intolerance, unintended weight loss and anxiety can occur. A clinician may assess TSH and other thyroid tests when appropriate.

When to seek help

Persistent symptoms deserve medical evaluation. Severe palpitations, chest pain, collapse or confusion require urgent assessment.
