Persistent Fever and Infections

Fever can accompany many infections and inflammatory illnesses, and its cause depends on the history and examination.

Symptoms and signs

A fever lasting several days, recurring fever, night sweats, cough, urinary symptoms or unexplained weight loss warrants clinical assessment. Avoid assuming every fever is typhoid or malaria.

Diagnosis and medical follow-up

The clinician may consider examination and targeted testing based on symptoms, exposures and local disease patterns. Antibiotics should not be started or continued without appropriate clinical advice.

When to seek urgent care

Fever with confusion, stiff neck, severe breathlessness, low blood pressure or dehydration requires urgent care.
