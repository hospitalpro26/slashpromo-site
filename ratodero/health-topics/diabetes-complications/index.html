Diabetes Complications and Follow-up

Diabetes can affect the eyes, kidneys, nerves, heart, blood vessels and feet over time.

Symptoms and signs

Warning signs may include reduced vision, foot numbness, persistent wounds and episodes of very high or low blood sugar. Many complications develop silently.

Diagnosis and medical follow-up

Regular individualized diabetes review may include HbA1c, kidney checks, eye examination, foot assessment, blood pressure and medicine review.

When to seek urgent care

Confusion, unconsciousness, vomiting with deep breathing, or an infected foot with spreading redness and fever requires urgent care.
