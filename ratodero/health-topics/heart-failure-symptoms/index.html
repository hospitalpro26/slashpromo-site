Heart Failure Warning Signs

Heart failure occurs when the heart cannot pump or fill effectively enough to meet the body's needs.

Symptoms and signs

Symptoms can include breathlessness on exertion or when lying down, ankle swelling, fatigue and sudden weight gain from fluid retention. Other conditions can produce similar symptoms.

Diagnosis and medical follow-up

A doctor may evaluate blood pressure, heart and lung findings, medicines and the need for tests such as ECG or echocardiography. Treatment depends on the diagnosis and heart function.

When to seek urgent care

New severe breathlessness, chest pain, fainting or pink frothy sputum requires immediate emergency care.
