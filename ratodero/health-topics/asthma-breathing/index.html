Asthma and Breathing Problems

Asthma may cause episodes of wheezing, cough, chest tightness and shortness of breath.

Symptoms and signs

Triggers can include dust, smoke, cold air and respiratory infections. Not all breathlessness is asthma; heart disease, lung infections and other problems must also be considered.

Diagnosis and medical follow-up

A doctor may assess symptoms, examine the chest and recommend breathing tests when appropriate. Inhaler technique and a written action plan may be discussed when asthma is confirmed.

When to seek urgent care

Severe breathlessness, blue lips, inability to speak in sentences or drowsiness is an emergency.
