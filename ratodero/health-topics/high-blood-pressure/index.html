High Blood Pressure (Hypertension) in Ratodero

Blood pressure checks, ongoing management and symptoms that need emergency attention.

Why checking blood pressure matters

Hypertension often causes no noticeable symptoms. Reliable repeated measurements help a clinician assess whether blood pressure is persistently elevated.

Medical follow-up

A clinician may assess cardiovascular risk, kidney health, existing medicines, diet and activity. Avoid stopping prescribed blood pressure medication without advice.

Emergency warning signs

Chest pain, severe shortness of breath, new weakness, speech difficulty or sudden vision changes require emergency medical attention. Do not wait for a routine clinic appointment.
