Gastritis and Abdominal Pain

Upper abdominal burning, indigestion or nausea can arise from several conditions and should not automatically be called gastritis.

Symptoms and signs

Symptoms may relate to reflux, ulcers, medication effects, gallbladder problems or other causes. The pattern, duration, medicines and warning symptoms help guide assessment.

Diagnosis and medical follow-up

A clinician may review diet, medicines and possible tests depending on the presentation. Persistent symptoms, swallowing difficulty or unexplained weight loss need a medical review.

When to seek urgent care

Severe sudden abdominal pain, vomiting blood, black stools or a rigid abdomen warrants emergency care.
