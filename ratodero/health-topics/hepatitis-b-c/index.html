Hepatitis B and C Symptoms

Hepatitis B and C can cause long-term liver inflammation, and some people have no symptoms for years.

Symptoms and signs

Possible symptoms include fatigue, loss of appetite, nausea, dark urine and yellowing of the eyes or skin. These are not specific to hepatitis; blood testing is needed to establish infection.

Diagnosis and medical follow-up

A clinician can discuss appropriate hepatitis B or C screening, interpretation of results, liver assessment, vaccination against hepatitis B and referral for antiviral treatment when indicated.

When to seek urgent care

Yellowing of the eyes with confusion, vomiting blood, black stools or a very swollen abdomen needs urgent medical attention.
