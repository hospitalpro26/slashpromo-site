Which concerns might lead someone to seek physiotherapy?

People may seek an assessment for common musculoskeletal complaints such as back pain, sciatica or shoulder stiffness, or when a clinician recommends rehabilitation after a fracture, injury or surgery. Treatment depends on individual medical history and examination; severe symptoms or warning signs may call for a doctor or emergency assessment instead.
