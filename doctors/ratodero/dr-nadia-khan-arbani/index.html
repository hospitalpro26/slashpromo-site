About Dr. Nadia Khan Arbani

Dr. Nadia Khan Arbani is a consultant physician practising at Barkat Clinic in Ratodero, Larkana District, Sindh. Her published clinical experience includes Chandka Medical College Hospital, NICVD Larkana, Civil Hospital Larkana and the HIV Centre at THQ Hospital Ratodero.
