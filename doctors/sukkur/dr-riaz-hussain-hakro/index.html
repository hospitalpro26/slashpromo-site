About Dr. Riaz Hussain Hakro

Dr. Riaz Hussain Hakro (MBBS, FCPS) is a Consultant Pulmonologist and Chest Specialist practising at Hira Medical Centre in Barrage Colony, Sukkur. His published professional profile describes more than 20 years of clinical experience and his academic role as Assistant Professor of Pulmonology at Gambat Institute of Medical Sciences (GIMS). His work encompasses respiratory medicine, specialist teaching and postgraduate supervision. His Slashcure listing also contains clinics outside Sukkur; choose the correct city and clinic when viewing current OPD schedules.

For asthma, COPD, chronic cough and other chest concerns, see his published weekly OPD schedule on Slashcure. Clinic sessions, consultation fees and availability can change, so verify before visiting.

Chest and respiratory conditions

His published areas of expertise include asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), chronic cough, pneumonia, tuberculosis, bronchiectasis, interstitial lung disease, pleural effusion and sleep-related breathing disorders. Patients with new or worsening breathlessness require appropriate clinical assessment rather than relying on online descriptions alone.
