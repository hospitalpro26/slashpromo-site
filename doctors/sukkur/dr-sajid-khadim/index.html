About Dr. Sajid Khadim

Dr. Sajid Khadim is a Paediatrician and Child Specialist associated with Dr. Ziauddin Hospital on Shikarpur Road, Sukkur. His published qualifications include MBBS, FCPS (Paediatrics) and PGPN Pro, with over nine years of experience in newborn, child and adolescent care. His background includes work in NICU, PICU, paediatric emergency care and teaching-hospital settings, including Dr. Ziauddin Hospital, Children's Hospital Sukkur and GMMMC Teaching Hospital. His Slashcure profile also lists a separate consultation location at Dua Medical Centre in Pano Aqil; check the correct clinic before making an appointment.

Clinical experience and areas of care

  • General paediatric consultations, vaccination, growth and developmental assessments
  • Newborn and premature infant care, neonatal infections and respiratory distress
  • Paediatric emergencies, NICU and PICU experience, resuscitation and parental counselling
