About Dr. Ammara Bhatti (PT)

Dr. Ammara Bhatti (PT) is a Consultant Physiotherapist and Musculoskeletal Rehabilitation Specialist practising at Dr. Shahid Mirani Hospital, Teer Chowk, Sukkur. Her published qualifications are DPT (JPMC), MPhil in Rehabilitation Sciences and certification in Clinical Dry Needling from the UK. She provides assessment and rehabilitation programmes for musculoskeletal pain, mobility limitations and neurological recovery needs. Her public Slashcure profile reports 5+ years of experience. Please confirm current OPD sessions directly before visiting.

Physiotherapy and rehabilitation services in Sukkur

Her published areas of care include lower-back and neck pain, joint pain and arthritis, frozen shoulder, sciatica, sports injuries, post-fracture or post-operative rehabilitation, stroke rehabilitation, posture correction and balance training. Treatments described on her profile include individual exercise plans, manual therapy, mobility training and clinical dry needling when appropriate.
