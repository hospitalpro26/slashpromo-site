About Surgeon Dr. Aftab Ahmed Mughal

Dr. Aftab Ahmed Mughal (MBBS, FCPS) is a senior consultant general and laparoscopic surgeon practising in Sukkur, Sindh, Pakistan. His published profile describes more than 20 years of clinical and surgical experience and over 5,000 laparoscopic surgeries performed. He previously served as an Assistant Professor at Sheikh Zayed Medical College/Hospital in Rahim Yar Khan, contributing to surgical education and specialist patient care.

His areas of clinical practice include general and minimally invasive abdominal surgery, emergency and trauma surgery, and surgical oncology. His reported experience covers difficult abdominal emergencies such as intestinal obstruction and perforation, traumatic and firearm-related injuries of the chest or abdomen, and selected breast, thyroid and intestinal cancer operations. The specific procedure and suitability of surgery require an individualized consultation.
