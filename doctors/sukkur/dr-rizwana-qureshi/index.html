Paediatric and child health consultations

Consult a paediatrician for assessment of childhood fever, cough, common infections, feeding and nutrition concerns, growth concerns and other child-health symptoms. The appropriate evaluation and management depend on the child's age and clinical condition; specific services should be confirmed with the doctor.
