Physiotherapy services in Sukkur

Conditions and recovery goals physiotherapy may help with

A qualified physiotherapist can assess relevant movement-related needs and suggest a plan suited to the patient's condition. Specific treatments and techniques depend on each clinician's scope, assessment and the clinic's facilities.

01

Back, neck & spine

Discuss persistent back or neck discomfort, posture problems and activity-related symptoms with a qualified professional.

02

Joint pain & stiffness

Explore physiotherapy assessment for selected joint problems, arthritis-related movement limitations and recovery.

03

Sports injuries

Physiotherapy plans can help address strength, mobility and return-to-activity goals after injury.

04

Neurological rehabilitation

Selected neurological conditions may benefit from structured movement, gait or balance rehabilitation within a medical plan.

05

Post-operative & post-injury recovery

Depending on surgical instructions and assessment, rehabilitation may help restore movement and daily function.

06

Therapeutic exercise & mobility

Individualized exercise, strengthening and functional training may support better movement and independence.

For severe injury, new limb weakness, sudden speech problems, severe unexplained pain or new bladder/bowel dysfunction, seek urgent medical assessment rather than routine physiotherapy.
