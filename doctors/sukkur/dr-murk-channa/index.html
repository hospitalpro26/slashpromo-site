Clinical background & gastroenterology care

Dr. Murk Channa is a consultant gastroenterologist, hepatologist and physician in Sukkur. Her published professional background includes clinical experience at Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre (JPMC), Karachi. Her work focuses on stomach and intestinal disorders, liver disease, hepatitis, acid reflux, digestive bleeding and the evaluation of patients who may require upper GI endoscopy. She is described in her published profile as Sukkur’s first female consultant gastroenterologist and hepatologist.

Specialist focus: Female Gastroenterologist, Hepatologist & Consultant Physician. Years in practice: 7+ years as displayed on Slashcure.
