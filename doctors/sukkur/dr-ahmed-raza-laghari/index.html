About Dr. Ahmed Raza Laghari

Dr. Ahmed Raza Laghari is a Consultant Orthopedic Surgeon at Hira Medical Centre, Barrage Colony, Sukkur, and an Assistant Professor at GMMMC Teaching Hospital. His published qualifications include MBBS, FCPS and a Fellowship in Ilizarov Surgery from Lecco, Italy. His public profile reports over 15 years of experience in orthopedic surgery, with special interests in difficult fracture healing, non-union fractures, limb reconstruction and deformity correction.

Areas of care and specialist services

  • Complex, delayed-union and non-union fractures and second opinions for failed fixation
  • Ilizarov external fixation, limb reconstruction and deformity correction
  • Orthopedic trauma assessment, bone infection and postoperative follow-up
