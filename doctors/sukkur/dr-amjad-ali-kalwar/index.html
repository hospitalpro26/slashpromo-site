About Dr. Amjad Ali Kalwar

Dr. Amjad Ali Kalwar holds MBBS and RMP credentials and works as a senior postgraduate trainee in Internal Medicine in Medicine Unit-I at Ghulam Muhammad Mahar Medical Teaching Hospital, Sukkur, near the Regional Blood Centre. His published background includes a 2021 medical graduation, an award for house-job performance, clinical experience at PUMHS Nawabshah and prior administration at RHC Dadloi. He provides supervised and team-based medical care as part of postgraduate training. The title postgraduate trainee should not be confused with that of a qualified consultant physician.

Clinical experience and areas of care

  • General internal medicine evaluation within the hospital's postgraduate medical team
  • Medical OPD assessment, chronic illness follow-up and coordinated investigations
  • Hospital and rural-health clinical practice; published details include RHC Dadloi in Ghotki district
