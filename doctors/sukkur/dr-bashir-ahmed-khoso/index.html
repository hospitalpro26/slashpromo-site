About Dr. Bashir Ahmed Khoso

Dr. Bashir Ahmed Khoso is a Consultant Rheumatologist and Physician in Sukkur, Sindh, Pakistan, with MBBS, FCPS (Medicine) and FCPS (Rheumatology) qualifications. His published professional profile describes more than 17 years of experience and an academic role as Assistant Professor at Gambat Institute of Medical Sciences (GIMS), Gambat.

At River City Hospital, Sukkur, his listed clinical focus includes the assessment and ongoing treatment of inflammatory arthritis, autoimmune and connective tissue disorders, vasculitis, gout, osteoporosis, and complex joint and muscle symptoms. His training in rheumatology and internal medicine helps patients seeking specialist evaluation of persistent pain, swelling, morning stiffness and multi-system inflammatory conditions.
