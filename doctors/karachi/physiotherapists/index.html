Physiotherapy and rehabilitation

What can a physiotherapist help you with?

Looking for a physiotherapy clinic in DHA Phase VI? Explore Astro Physiocare Karachi, including treatment services, clinic locations and its associated physiotherapy team.

After an appropriate assessment, physiotherapists may provide individualized care for back or neck pain, joint stiffness, posture and movement problems, sports injuries, balance difficulties, and recovery after selected injuries or operations. Available treatments vary by therapist and clinic.

Musculoskeletal rehabilitation

Assessment-led support for back pain, neck discomfort, joint mobility and everyday physical function.

Sports injury recovery

Exercise-based rehabilitation and gradual return-to-activity planning following suitable clinical evaluation.

Post-injury and post-operative rehabilitation

Tailored movement and strengthening plans when recommended by the treating clinical team.

Mobility, posture and functional exercise

Support for strength, balance, flexibility and safer movement patterns.

For sudden weakness, serious trauma, chest pain or other emergency symptoms, seek urgent medical care rather than relying on a directory search.
