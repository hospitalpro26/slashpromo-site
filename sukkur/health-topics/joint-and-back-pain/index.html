Understanding joint, bone & back pain

Joint or back pain can have many causes. Inflammatory joint disorders such as rheumatoid arthritis or gout may need rheumatology assessment, while injuries and structural conditions may require orthopedic evaluation. Depending on the circumstances, a healthcare professional may evaluate injury history, mobility, strength and other symptoms before recommending the next step.
