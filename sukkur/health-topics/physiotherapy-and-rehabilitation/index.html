Understanding physiotherapy & rehabilitation

Physiotherapy may support recovery of movement, strength and daily function after injury or illness. The appropriate plan depends on assessment, diagnosis and individual needs.

Questions to ask at an appointment

  • Which functional difficulties should I explain?
  • Is medical referral or imaging needed before starting rehabilitation?
  • Which treatments, session fees and follow-up schedules does the clinic provide?

When to seek urgent medical attention: New major weakness, sudden neurological symptoms or loss of bladder or bowel control requires urgent medical evaluation before routine rehabilitation.
