Understanding stomach, digestive & liver symptoms

Digestive symptoms may include reflux, abdominal discomfort, bowel habit changes or suspected liver conditions. A clinician can advise whether investigation or referral is necessary; procedures are not required for every symptom.

Questions to ask at an appointment

  • How long have the symptoms been present and what worsens them?
  • Should I bring previous liver tests, ultrasound reports or prescriptions?
  • If investigation is advised, where is it offered and what are the arrangements?

When to seek urgent medical attention: Vomiting blood, passing black stools, severe sudden abdominal pain or major dehydration warrants urgent medical assessment.
