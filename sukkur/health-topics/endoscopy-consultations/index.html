Warning signs — seek urgent medical care

Vomiting blood, black tarry stools with weakness or fainting, or sudden severe abdominal pain needs urgent assessment in an emergency department, not routine outpatient endoscopy scheduling.

Frequently asked questions about endoscopy in Sukkur

Which symptoms should make me ask about endoscopy? / اینڈوسکوپی کی ضرورت کی علامات کیا ہیں؟

Persistent acidity despite treatment, ongoing upper abdominal pain, difficulty swallowing, repeated vomiting, unexpected weight loss or unexplained iron-deficiency anaemia should be assessed by a gastroenterologist. Read the complete English–Urdu symptoms list above ↗. Bleeding or sudden severe pain requires urgent care.

Do chronic acidity or stomach pain always require endoscopy?

No. Your doctor will consider symptoms, age, medical history and warning signs before recommending tests. An endoscopy is not necessary for everyone with reflux or indigestion.

Is upper GI endoscopy a painful procedure?

Usually not. Before upper GI endoscopy, the throat may be numbed with a local anaesthetic spray or gargle, and an intravenous sedative injection may be given when appropriate to help you relax. Some people still experience brief gagging, pressure or bloating. Sedation is not always used, and the exact approach depends on the patient and facility. Ask your endoscopy team about pain control, sedation and safety before the procedure.

What is the difference between endoscopy and colonoscopy?

Upper GI endoscopy examines the upper digestive tract, including the stomach. Colonoscopy examines the large intestine. They investigate different problems.
