Understanding thyroid symptoms

The thyroid gland helps regulate the body's metabolism. An underactive or overactive thyroid can cause varied symptoms, many of which overlap with other conditions. A doctor may use a history, examination and blood tests to investigate.

Symptoms and questions for your doctor

  • An underactive thyroid may cause fatigue, feeling cold, constipation, dry skin or weight gain.
  • An overactive thyroid may cause heat intolerance, palpitations, tremor, sweating or unexplained weight loss.
  • Symptoms are not specific; a clinician may consider TSH and thyroid hormone tests such as free T4.
  • Treatment varies by diagnosis. Do not start thyroid medicine based on symptoms or online advice alone.

Frequently asked questions

Can thyroid symptoms be diagnosed without tests?

Symptoms overlap with other illnesses; clinicians often use TSH and thyroid hormone blood tests.

Which doctor should I consult first?

A consultant physician can assess common symptoms and refer to an endocrinologist if specialist care is needed.
