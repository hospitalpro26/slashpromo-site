Services and practical patient information

When might home physiotherapy be helpful?

An at-home assessment may be convenient for selected patients with difficulty walking, reduced strength, joint stiffness, or rehabilitation needs after injury or surgery. Some conditions need investigation or hospital care before physiotherapy can begin.

What happens during an initial assessment?

A physiotherapist may discuss symptoms, medical history, movement, pain, daily activities and rehabilitation goals. Exercises or techniques should be individualized to medical advice and physical findings rather than prescribed from a webpage.

Rehabilitation following illness or surgery

Dr Komal's published experience covers musculoskeletal, orthopedic and neurological rehabilitation. Post-operative exercises must respect restrictions provided by the surgeon or treating clinician.

Check home visit coverage and fees

Her Slashcure listing mentions home visits and publishes consultation information, but availability and prices can change. Check the current service area, timings, fees and appointment confirmation directly on her profile before arranging a visit.

When home physiotherapy is not enough

New stroke-like symptoms, severe or worsening weakness, chest pain, difficulty breathing, a painful swollen leg, new loss of bladder or bowel control or fever following an operation require prompt medical attention, not a routine home session.
