Cholecystectomy and gallstones: quick explanation

Laparoscopic cholecystectomy is keyhole surgery to remove the gallbladder. It may be recommended for symptomatic gallstones or some gallbladder complications. Not all gallstones require surgery. A surgeon should review symptoms, ultrasound, medical history and the patient's individual risks to determine the appropriate care.

Seek urgent medical assessment for severe or persistent abdominal pain, fever, jaundice or repeated vomiting.

There is no guarantee of a pain-free operation, immediate recovery or suitability for every patient.
