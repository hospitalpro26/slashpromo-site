When should you seek urgent maternity assessment?

Severe abdominal pain, heavy vaginal bleeding, seizures, severe headache with vision changes, difficulty breathing, reduced fetal movements or concerns about labour require prompt medical evaluation. For emergencies, contact the hospital or the nearest equipped emergency service immediately.

Normal delivery, C-section and VBAC are not interchangeable packages; delivery plans depend on obstetric history, current pregnancy risks and clinician assessment.

Frequently asked questions

What is the C-section package price in Sukkur?

For the latest C-section package price and details of included charges, contact the hospital directly. Prices may vary according to individual care needs.

Does the hospital offer caesarean delivery?

Yes. C-section delivery is among the centre's maternity services. Contact the obstetric team to discuss availability and whether it is appropriate for your pregnancy.

What other maternity services are listed?

Services include antenatal care, high-risk pregnancy care, normal delivery, VBAC assessment, infertility care, recurrent miscarriage assessment and gynaecology consultations.

Are NICU and ultrasound facilities available?

NICU, ultrasound, laboratory, pharmacy, blood bank and surgical services are among the centre's facilities. Contact the hospital to confirm availability for your needs.

Where is the hospital in Sukkur?

Near NICVD Hospital, Bypass Road / main Sukkur–Shikarpur Road, Sukkur, Sindh, Pakistan. Contact the facility to confirm directions.

Can everyone choose a C-section or VBAC?

The appropriate mode of delivery depends on clinical evaluation, medical and obstetric history, and available emergency resources. Discuss benefits and risks with an obstetrician.
