Kidney stone treatment and lithotripsy services in Sukkur

Dr Ziauddin Hospital's official lithotripsy page lists stone treatment at its Sukkur and Karachi campuses. Published urology services include the following options; the appropriate treatment and availability for a particular patient require specialist confirmation.

ESWL / shock-wave lithotripsy

Focused shock waves may fragment selected kidney or ureteric stones without an incision.

Laser lithotripsy and ureteroscopy (URS)

Endoscopic approaches can locate and fragment urinary stones where appropriate.

Retrograde intrarenal surgery (RIRS)

Flexible endoscopic access to stones inside the kidney, often with laser treatment.

Percutaneous nephrolithotomy (PCNL)

A minimally invasive operation generally considered for large or complex kidney stones.

Which treatment might be suitable?

Some smaller stones pass with observation, pain management and follow-up. Larger or obstructing stones may need a procedure. Choice of ESWL, ureteroscopy, RIRS or PCNL depends on imaging, stone size and position, infection, kidney function and individual clinical factors.

When to get urgent care

Fever or chills with suspected stones, inability to pass urine, persistent vomiting or uncontrolled pain can signal a serious problem. Seek urgent medical evaluation rather than waiting for a routine appointment.

Frequently asked questions

Who treats kidney stones in Sukkur?

A consultant urologist assesses kidney or ureteric stones. Dr Naveed Ahmed Mahar is listed on Slashcure as a consultant urologist at Dr Ziauddin Hospital in Sukkur.

Is lithotripsy available in Sukkur?

Dr Ziauddin Hospital's official lithotripsy page lists services at its Sukkur and Karachi campuses. Contact the hospital to confirm current availability, fees and suitability.

Does every kidney stone need surgery?

No. Small stones may pass without a procedure. Treatment depends on stone size, location, infection, pain, obstruction and kidney function.

What is the difference between ESWL and laser treatment?

ESWL uses shock waves from outside the body; laser lithotripsy generally uses a scope within the urinary tract. Your urologist can assess the appropriate option.

When should I seek emergency care?

Fever with suspected kidney stones, inability to pass urine, severe uncontrolled pain, persistent vomiting or feeling very unwell requires urgent medical assessment.
