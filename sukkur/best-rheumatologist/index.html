What is the difference between a rheumatologist and an orthopedic surgeon?

Rheumatologists focus on inflammatory and autoimmune disorders affecting joints and connective tissues. Orthopedic surgeons assess musculoskeletal injuries and structural conditions, including problems that may require procedures. Your clinician can help determine which assessment is appropriate.
