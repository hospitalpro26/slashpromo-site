Knee pain and arthritis in Sukkur: orthopedic assessment guide

Knee pain can arise from osteoarthritis, injury, inflammation or infection. The right referral depends on the cause and on urgent warning signs.

Educational content only. This page cannot diagnose a condition or confirm a doctor's treatment availability.

What causes knee pain?

Possible causes include overuse, ligament or meniscus injury, osteoarthritis and inflammatory conditions. A hot, very swollen knee may signal infection and must be assessed urgently.

When should I seek advice?

Persistent or recurrent pain, swelling, difficulty with stairs or walking, reduced range of motion or instability are reasons to discuss assessment with a clinician.

Is surgery always needed?

No. Depending on the diagnosis, exercise-based rehabilitation, medication advice and other nonsurgical measures may be considered. Orthopedic referral can be appropriate for injury, structural damage or refractory symptoms.

Which doctor should I see?

The Sukkur orthopedic directory lists Dr. Ahmed Raza Laghari and Dr. Dost Muhammad. Inflammatory disease may need a rheumatologist; rehabilitation questions may be suitable for a physiotherapist after evaluation. The directory does not establish that a listed doctor offers a particular knee procedure.

When to seek urgent medical help

A hot swollen joint with fever, inability to bear weight, significant deformity or sudden severe pain needs urgent medical assessment.
