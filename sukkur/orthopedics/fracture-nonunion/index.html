Fracture not healing? Nonunion and delayed fracture healing in Sukkur

Persistent pain after a broken bone should not be ignored. Learn when fracture healing needs assessment and find published orthopedic surgeon profiles in Sukkur.

Educational content only. This page cannot diagnose a condition or confirm a doctor's treatment availability.

What is delayed union or nonunion?

A delayed union means a broken bone is taking longer than expected to heal. A nonunion means the fracture has not united. The diagnosis depends on clinical review and serial imaging—not pain alone.

What signs need an orthopedic review?

Ongoing pain at the fracture site, difficulty using the limb, persistent swelling, or lack of progress on repeat X-rays are reasons for reassessment. Bring earlier scans, operative notes and medication information.

What can affect bone healing?

Stability of the fracture, local blood supply, infection, tobacco use, nutrition and some conditions such as diabetes can affect healing. The care plan may involve further imaging and investigation.

What happens after assessment?

An orthopedic team may review fixation, alignment, infection risk and rehabilitation. Depending on the findings, management may be nonsurgical or involve bone grafting, internal fixation or external fixation. No specific procedure is guaranteed at a listed clinic.

When to seek urgent medical help

An open wound over a suspected fracture, rapidly worsening pain, a cold or numb limb, high fever, or severe new deformity after injury requires urgent hospital assessment.

No. Assessment and comparison of imaging over time are needed to establish why symptoms continue.
