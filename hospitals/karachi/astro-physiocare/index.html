About Astro Physiocare

Personalized physiotherapy and rehabilitation in Karachi

Astro Physiocare, also known as Astrophysiocare, is a Karachi-based physiotherapy and rehabilitation practice focused on movement recovery, musculoskeletal care and postural wellness. Founded by consultant physiotherapist Dr. Mahum Jamil (PT), the clinic describes an assessment-led approach, individualized rehabilitation exercises, practical guidance and follow-up according to each person's needs.

The organisation publishes services across spine and joint rehabilitation, sports injuries, neurological recovery, pelvic-floor and women's health physiotherapy, geriatric and chest physiotherapy, as well as manual therapy, traction and selected physical treatment modalities. Treatments should be selected after clinical assessment rather than as a one-size-fits-all package.

In addition to physiotherapy sessions, the clinic has described home visits in selected Karachi areas, online physiotherapy consultations, posture education and workplace ergonomic training. Confirm the current availability of these options directly with the clinic.

What Astro Physiocare offers

Physiotherapy services, rehabilitation and treatment areas

The following therapies and rehabilitation categories are based on the centre's published services. They are not promises of a particular result, and some are appropriate only for selected patients.

01

Spine, posture & musculoskeletal care

Assessment-led care for back and neck pain, joint movement, muscle stiffness, postural problems and functional mobility.

  • Musculoskeletal physiotherapy and rehabilitation
  • Low-back pain and neck pain rehabilitation
  • Posture correction and ergonomics
  • Cervical traction and lumbar traction
  • Joint mobility and soft tissue manual therapy
02

Sports, strength & recovery

Individual rehabilitation plans to restore movement, strength and everyday activity after appropriate clinical evaluation.

  • Sports injury rehabilitation
  • Full-body and muscle rehabilitation
  • Therapeutic exercises and strength training
  • Post-operative rehabilitation
  • Pneumatic compression therapy when indicated
03

Neurological & age-related rehabilitation

Targeted care for movement and balance difficulties, within the clinician's training and the patient's medical plan.

  • Stroke and neurological rehabilitation
  • Gait, mobility and balance training
  • Geriatric rehabilitation
  • Respiratory and chest physiotherapy
04

Women's health & pelvic-floor care

Physiotherapy support for selected needs in pregnancy, after childbirth and during later life.

  • Pregnancy and postpartum rehabilitation
  • Pelvic floor rehabilitation
  • Women's health physiotherapy
05

Other techniques and pain modalities

Additional approaches advertised by the clinic; use depends on individual assessment, informed consent and suitability.

  • Dry needling
  • Dry cupping and dynamic cupping
  • Hijama (wet cupping)
  • Pain-alleviation physical modalities

Dry needling, cupping and traction have specific suitability and safety considerations. Ask the treating clinician about likely benefits, risks, alternatives and whether the service is appropriate for your condition. Available devices, staff and appointments can vary by branch.
