Alzheimer's Blood Test: FDA Clears Roche pTau217 Assessment in 2026

Roche announced US FDA clearance for the Elecsys pTau217 plasma blood test as an aid to assessing amyloid pathology in symptomatic adults aged 55 or older.

What was cleared

Roche announced on 24 August 2026 that the US FDA had cleared its Elecsys Phospho-Tau (217P) Plasma blood test. FDA documentation lists device K261686. It measures a blood biomarker associated with amyloid pathology and is indicated as an aid in assessment for certain adults aged 55 years or older who have cognitive concerns.

Who is the test intended for?

The company describes its use for people aged at least 55 with signs, symptoms or complaints of cognitive decline. It is not intended as a general screening test for symptom-free people of every age. Clinical history, neurological assessment and the patient's wider condition remain essential.

Does a blood test diagnose Alzheimer's by itself?

No single laboratory result replaces a full medical assessment. A biomarker can help assess the likelihood of Alzheimer's-associated amyloid pathology, but clinicians must interpret it alongside other findings, alternative causes of cognitive impairment and applicable diagnostic pathways.

Availability in Pakistan

The documented regulatory event is US FDA clearance, not confirmation that the test is available in Pakistan or is appropriate for a particular person. Families should consult appropriately trained clinicians about memory concerns.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a general Alzheimer's screening blood test?

No. Its intended population includes symptomatic adults aged 55 and over.

Does a positive result establish dementia on its own?

No. A healthcare professional should interpret biomarkers alongside symptoms, examination and other investigations.

When was the US clearance announced?

Roche announced FDA clearance on 24 August 2026.
