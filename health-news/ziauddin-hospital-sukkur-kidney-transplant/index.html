SUKKUR: A Facebook post attributed to Dr. Naveed Ahmed Mahar has reported a kidney transplant performed at Dr. Ziauddin Hospital Sukkur, a private healthcare facility in northern Sindh. The accompanying announcement identifies Dr. Naveed Ahmed Mahar as a transplant surgeon and describes the procedure as successful.

The image accompanying the post also features Dr. Hayat Khan Kakar. The precise responsibilities of each member of the transplant team, the surgical date, and independently verified clinical outcomes were not provided in the screenshot shared with SlashPromo.

The visible Facebook post is labelled 18 September, without a year visible in the supplied screenshot. This article is dated 21 November 2026 and does not assume that the post date is the exact date of the operation.

Verification note: The transplant and reported outcome are attributed to the Facebook announcement and the public Slashcure profile. We could not independently verify the operation record, long-term patient outcome, or whether this was the first kidney transplant carried out by a private hospital in Sukkur.

What does the “first kidney transplant” claim mean?

The social media artwork describes the procedure as a landmark transplant for Sukkur. It is important not to interpret that statement as the first-ever kidney transplant in the city: the Sindh Institute of Urology and Transplantation (SIUT) reports that kidney transplantation began at its Sukkur facility in 2019, a milestone also documented by the Express Tribune.

The Ziauddin announcement may therefore represent a reported private-sector milestone, rather than the first kidney transplant in Sukkur overall. Independent confirmation of that narrower private-hospital distinction remains outstanding.

Role and professional profile of the urologist

According to his verified Slashcure profile, Dr. Naveed Ahmed Mahar is an Associate Professor and Section Head of Urology and Robotics at Dr. Ziauddin Hospital, Sukkur. His listed qualifications are MBBS, FCPS (Urology), FACS, MRCS and CHPE, and his profile records more than nine years of experience.

His documented interests include kidney transplantation, advanced endourology, stone surgery, reconstructive urology and robotic urological procedures. The profile also records earlier experience with SIUT. For current credentials, consultation arrangements, and services, refer directly to Dr. Naveed Ahmed Mahar's Slashcure profile.

Why this matters for people in Sukkur and northern Sindh

Availability of specialist transplant expertise in a private hospital could provide another route to referral and specialist assessment for appropriate patients in Sukkur and nearby cities. However, the social media announcement alone does not confirm a sustained transplant programme, service eligibility, prices, waiting times, or current treatment availability.

Kidney transplantation requires careful medical assessment, donor and recipient suitability checks, regulatory and ethical safeguards, and long-term follow-up. Patients should consult an appropriately qualified transplant team for individual advice.
