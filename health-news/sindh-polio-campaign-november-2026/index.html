Sindh Polio Drive 2026: 7.104 Million Children Vaccinated in November

Pakistan's official Polio Eradication Initiative reported that approximately 7.104 million children received oral polio vaccine across 20 selected Sindh districts in the 2–8 November 2026 sub-national campaign.

What the official programme reported

The Pakistan Polio Eradication Initiative reported more than 24 million children under five vaccinated nationally during its 2–8 November campaign across 97 districts, including about 7.104 million in 20 Sindh districts. These are campaign achievement figures announced on 12 November, not today's vaccination coverage. Sindh's Information Department had earlier announced a target of nearly 6.8 million children in 20 districts.

Why repeated vaccination is important

Polio can cause permanent paralysis. Oral polio vaccine helps prevent transmission and protects children. Repeated campaigns and routine childhood immunization are complementary, not substitutes. Caregivers should check their child's routine immunization record and follow the current schedule through an authorized vaccination service.

What should parents do now?

Parents whose eligible child missed a campaign visit should contact their local health team or an authorized vaccination centre for up-to-date guidance. Keep vaccine records and ask a qualified health worker when the next dose or campaign is due. Do not assume a specific campaign is running today based on this November report.
