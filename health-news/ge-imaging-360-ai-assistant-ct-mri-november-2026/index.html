New AI Assistant for CT and MRI Teams: GE HealthCare Imaging 360 Update

GE HealthCare introduced an Imaging 360 AI assistant and AI-assisted protocoling features for operational CT and MRI workflows.

Published 22 November 2026 · Source-attributed medical technology news

Published 22 November 2026. Reporting is based on the attributed sources below. This article provides general information, not medical advice, and does not imply the technology is available in Pakistan.

What was announced

On 20 November 2026, GE HealthCare announced two Imaging 360 capabilities: an AI assistant and AI-assisted protocoling. The features are designed for radiology teams managing CT and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) examinations and related workflows.

What the AI assistant does

The company describes a read-only natural-language assistant for exploring protocol-related analytics and answering operational questions. It does not interpret individual patient scans, provide patient-specific clinical recommendations, or alter original protocol data.

What AI-assisted protocoling means

Protocoling is the process of selecting the appropriate imaging approach for an examination request. The software is described as offering recommendations for radiologists and technologists to review before they select a protocol in their radiology information system. It does not replace qualified radiology staff.

What readers should know

This is a vendor announcement of workflow functionality, including technology planned for demonstration at RSNA 2026. It does not establish independent patient-outcome benefit or implementation at Pakistani hospitals.
