AI X-Ray Software Helps Detect Feeding Tube Position: GE HealthCare's 2026 Update

GE HealthCare announced US FDA 510(k) clearance for on-device AI detecting feeding tubes and visualizing their position in adult and paediatric mobile X-rays.

Published 22 November 2026 · Source-attributed medical technology news

Published 22 November 2026. Reporting is based on the attributed sources below. This article provides general information, not medical advice, and does not imply the technology is available in Pakistan.

What was announced

On 17 November 2026, GE HealthCare announced that its Critical Care Suite 3.0 had received US FDA 510(k) clearance. The company describes on-device artificial intelligence designed to detect enteric feeding tubes on mobile X-ray images and help clinical teams assess tube path, tip and side-port location. The update includes support for paediatric patients as well as adults.

How the technology works

The software operates on compatible mobile X-ray equipment without relying on internet access or a cloud service for the AI function. It highlights relevant tube anatomy and structures such as the diaphragm and airways. The aim is to make image-based information more readily available near the bedside, especially in intensive care or emergency settings.

What the evidence does—and does not—show

GE HealthCare reported tube detection sensitivity of 99.2% in its stated testing, and a mean DICE localization figure of 91.6%. These are company-reported technical performance figures, not proof that every hospital achieves identical results or that complications have been eliminated. Clinicians must still interpret the full radiograph and make the placement decision.

Relevance for Pakistan

The US clearance relates to a medical-device software product; it does not establish Pakistani regulatory authorization, hospital availability or pricing. Local radiology and critical-care teams may be interested in the approach as medical AI develops.

Frequently asked questions

Can AI confirm feeding tube placement without a clinician?

No. The software provides decision support; clinicians remain responsible for assessing the full image and patient context.

Does the system need cloud internet access?

GE HealthCare describes its feeding-tube AI as running on the mobile X-ray device.

Is it available in Pakistan?

The cited announcement confirms US FDA clearance, not availability or authorization in Pakistan.
