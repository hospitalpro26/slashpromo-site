Karachi's SMBB Institute of Trauma Provides 24/7 Emergency and Critical Care Services

The Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Institute of Trauma (SMBBIT) in Saddar, Karachi, describes round-the-clock emergency and trauma services. A sponsored SAMAA TV video also highlights its emergency care.

22 November 2026 · SlashPromo Health Desk · Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan

Emergency and trauma care at SMBBIT Karachi

Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Institute of Trauma on Chand Bibi Road, Saddar, Karachi, provides 24-hour emergency and trauma services, according to the institute's official website. The public-sector institution describes multidisciplinary care for seriously injured patients, with emergency assessment, resuscitation, surgery and critical-care support.

A sponsored SAMAA TV Facebook feature highlights SMBBIT's round-the-clock emergency service. The video caption establishes the subject of the feature; the details below come from the institute's published service information, not a transcription of the programme.

What emergency services are available?

🚑 24/7 accident and emergency care

SMBBIT describes round-the-clock trauma assessment, resuscitation and emergency management.

🏥 116-bed emergency department

The institute's Emergency Medicine Department describes a 116-bed emergency facility with dedicated assessment and critical-care areas.

🩻 Imaging and diagnostics

CT scanning, X-ray, ultrasound, laboratory and pharmacy support are described on the official departmental pages.

🩺 Specialist surgical care

The institute lists neurosurgery, orthopaedics, vascular surgery, oral and maxillofacial surgery, anaesthesiology and other trauma-related specialties.

🫀 Critical care and rehabilitation

Published hospital services include intensive and high-dependency care, with rehabilitation support for suitable patients.

SMBBIT also describes a dedicated medico-legal department and specialised emergency support services. The institute's published information does not establish current bed availability or waiting times.

Why trauma response matters

Road collisions, serious falls, head injuries and other major trauma may require rapid stabilisation, investigations and specialist surgery. Coordinated emergency teams can support timely assessment and treatment when injuries involve more than one organ system.

When to seek immediate help

For major injuries, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, breathing difficulty or suspected spinal injury, seek emergency medical assistance immediately. Do not delay care while searching for online doctor listings.
