🏥 Emergency and trauma care at SMBBIT Karachi

Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Institute of Trauma on Chand Bibi Road, Saddar, Karachi, provides 24-hour emergency and trauma services, according to the institute's official website. The public-sector institution describes multidisciplinary care for seriously injured patients, with emergency assessment, resuscitation, surgery and critical-care support.

A sponsored SAMAA TV Facebook feature highlights SMBBIT's round-the-clock emergency service. The video caption establishes the subject of the feature; the details below come from the institute's published service information, not a transcription of the programme.

Why trauma response matters

Road collisions, serious falls, head injuries and other major trauma may require rapid stabilisation, investigations and specialist surgery. Coordinated emergency teams can support timely assessment and treatment when injuries involve more than one organ system.

When to seek immediate help

For major injuries, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, breathing difficulty or suspected spinal injury, seek emergency medical assistance immediately. Do not delay care while searching for online doctor listings.
