Robotic Surgery in Karachi: JPMC Experience and Ziauddin's New Programme

Media reports describe JPMC's robotic surgery programme and a new private-hospital launch at Dr Ziauddin Hospital Karachi; procedure counts are hospital-reported.

Published 22 November 2026 · Source-attributed medical technology news

Published 22 November 2026. Reporting is based on the attributed sources below. This article provides general information, not medical advice, and does not imply the technology is available in Pakistan.

What happened in Karachi

A September 2026 report in The Express Tribune quoted Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre (JPMC) personnel as saying the hospital had performed more than 1,500 robotic procedures since starting its programme in October 2023. The figure is attributed to the hospital report; it was not independently audited for this article. Sindh's own budget material confirms that robotic surgery facilities were introduced at JPMC.

A new programme at Dr Ziauddin Hospital

In early October 2026, reporting attributed to Dr Ziauddin Hospital described the launch of a robotic-assisted surgery programme in Karachi. Hospital representatives discussed surgeon training and uses of minimally invasive technology. This refers to the Karachi facility and should not be confused with the separately listed Dr Ziauddin Hospital in Sukkur.

What robotic-assisted surgery means

In robotic-assisted surgery, a trained surgeon controls instruments through a robotic system. The machine does not independently operate on patients. Depending on the procedure and patient, minimally invasive techniques may reduce incision size, but outcomes depend on surgical indication, expertise and available facilities. Not every condition requires a robotic operation.

What patients should verify

Patients should ask the treating hospital whether a particular procedure is currently offered, the surgeon's experience, alternatives, possible complications, and cost or eligibility. Historical reports do not confirm today's OPD schedule or service availability.
