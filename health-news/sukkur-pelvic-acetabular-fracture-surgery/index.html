Pelvic and Acetabular Fracture Surgery Reported at Sukkur Trauma Centre

An attributed social media report describes a complex fracture-fixation procedure and the orthopaedic team involved. The operation has not been independently verified by SlashPromo.

Published 21 November 2026SlashPromo Health DeskSource: Abdul Wajid Wadho on Facebook

According to a Facebook post attributed to Abdul Wajid Wadho, an orthopaedic team at the Trauma and Emergency Response Centre Sukkur performed surgery for a patient with pelvic and acetabular fractures.

The post says the patient sustained injuries after falling from a train and underwent medical assessment and imaging. It reports that a surgical team subsequently used reconstructive plates and screws to treat the fractures. Details of the operation, the date it took place, and the patient's recovery have not been independently confirmed.

Reporting note: This article describes claims made in a social media post, not an official statement from the hospital. The report described the operation as a first for the centre, but SlashPromo has not verified that claim and does not present it as established fact.

Orthopaedic team named in the report

The Facebook account lists the following clinicians as members of the surgical team:

  • Professor Dr. Zulfiqar Soomro — Orthopaedic Surgeon
  • Dr. Rashed Mahmood Soomro — Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon
  • Dr. Aziz Ullah Bhai — Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon
  • Dr. Saqib Shah — Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon
  • Dr. Raja Lakhiyar — Orthopaedic Surgeon

These names and professional roles are reproduced as attributed information from the post; their exact spellings and involvement have not been separately confirmed. The source also acknowledges Dr. Mahmood Qureshi for administrative coordination and states that Dr. Rashed Mahmood Soomro travelled from Larkana to assist.

Why pelvic and acetabular injuries need specialist care

The acetabulum is the socket-shaped part of the pelvis that forms the hip joint. Certain pelvic and acetabular fractures may require careful surgical planning, fixation with specialist implants and rehabilitation. The appropriate treatment and outcome depend on the specific injury and the patient's clinical condition.

Recognition following the reported procedure

The social media account also describes an appreciation gathering in which members of the team were presented with traditional Sindhi Ajrak. SlashPromo has not independently verified the event details.
