Complex Pelvic and Acetabular Fracture Surgery at Sukkur Trauma Centre

An orthopaedic team at Sukkur Trauma Centre carried out a complex pelvic and acetabular fracture operation, using reconstructive plates and screws.

According to a Facebook post attributed to Abdul Wajid Wadho, an orthopaedic team at the Trauma and Emergency Response Centre Sukkur performed surgery for a patient with pelvic and acetabular fractures.

The patient sustained injuries after falling from a train and underwent assessment and imaging before the operation. The orthopaedic team used reconstructive plates and screws to stabilise the fractures. The available report does not provide a full account of the patient's subsequent recovery.

Orthopaedic milestone: The surgery marks an important example of complex fracture care at Sukkur Trauma Centre. The source post also describes it as a first for the centre.

Orthopaedic team named in the report

The Facebook account lists the following clinicians as members of the surgical team:

  • Professor Dr. Zulfiqar Soomro — Orthopaedic Surgeon
  • Dr. Rashed Mahmood Soomro — Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon
  • Dr. Aziz Ullah Bhai — Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon
  • Dr. Saqib Shah — Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon
  • Dr. Raja Lakhiyar — Orthopaedic Surgeon

The original account also acknowledges Dr. Mahmood Qureshi for administrative coordination and notes that Dr. Rashed Mahmood Soomro travelled from Larkana to assist the team.

Why pelvic and acetabular injuries need specialist care

The acetabulum is the socket-shaped part of the pelvis that forms the hip joint. Certain pelvic and acetabular fractures may require careful surgical planning, fixation with specialist implants and rehabilitation. The appropriate treatment and outcome depend on the specific injury and the patient's clinical condition.

Recognition following the surgery

Following the surgery, members of the team were honoured with traditional Sindhi Ajrak at an appreciation gathering, as shown in the shared photographs.
