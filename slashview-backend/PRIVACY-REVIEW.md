# Slashview privacy and consent — review draft
Not a published privacy policy. Verify actual processing and support contacts before launch.

## Registration disclosure
Slashview stores limited account information (name, email, identity verification status, password hash where applicable, approval, subscription and session records). Device authorization, payment references and consent records will be stored when those features are enabled. Personal library files, scanned books, notes, highlights and bookmarks remain local in the current app. AI requests may transmit selected excerpts and prompts to the configured AI provider only after separate explicit permission; provider processing and retention are governed by its applicable terms. Do not submit patient-identifiable data. No sale of personal information is permitted under the proposed policy.

## Required consent
Before activating either Google or password registration, display versioned Terms and Privacy Policy and trial/subscription disclosure. Require affirmative acceptance of terms and acknowledgment of privacy notice and pricing, with no prechecked boxes. Record policy versions and server timestamp on the server; enforce acceptance in registration APIs. Promotional messages require separate optional opt-in, revocable independently. Provide renewed notice when material terms change. No automatic billing at trial expiry under manual-payment model.

## Support
Official owner-designated support email: slashviewofficial@gmail.com. Add Help & Support, Contact Slashview Team via email, report a problem, FAQ, subscription help, privacy requests and account deletion request. Do not include WhatsApp links, buttons, phone-number fields or WhatsApp contact details in the application at this stage. Email contact should be voluntary and must not automatically include credentials, library contents or patient data. Verify mailbox delivery before launch.

## Before launch
Implement consent persistence and enforcement, deletion and retention workflows, recovery, verified support contact, policy publication, provider disclosure and privacy/security review. Do not promise zero data collection or absolute security. Preserve local books and notes when subscriptions expire or accounts are deleted. Clarify that AI output is educational, may be inaccurate, and is not clinical advice. Users must have permission to import their materials.
