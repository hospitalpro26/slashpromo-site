About SlashPromo

A new way to showcase healthcare.

SlashPromo is a promotional media platform for healthcare organizations and professionals across Pakistan, welcoming healthcare providers from every city.

Our mission

Make healthcare stories clear, creative and easier to discover.

SlashPromo is designed to highlight the people, institutions and services that shape healthcare in Pakistan. Instead of attempting to replace clinical directories, we create campaign-led showcases that point visitors to published information about a provider.

SlashPromo welcomes campaign enquiries from healthcare professionals and organizations in every Pakistani city. Published city pages showcase existing material, but they do not limit where campaigns may be created. We publish location-specific content when accurate information is available.

Our relationship with Slashcure

SlashPromo is associated with Slashcure.com. The platforms have different roles. SlashPromo focuses on creative promotion and discovery. Slashcure is a healthcare platform for doctor profiles, hospitals and patient-facing tools. SlashPromo does not itself provide clinical consultations or guarantee appointment availability.

A clear distinction: Some showcases are editorial examples and some may become paid placements. We label sponsorship when it applies and do not sell clinical rankings.

Our approach

We favor useful descriptions, clear links, permissioned visuals, transparent advertising, and practical information over misleading superlatives. Providers can request corrections or removals through our contact channel.
