What is sciatica?

Sciatica commonly causes pain travelling from the lower back or buttock down the back of a leg. Tingling, altered sensation or weakness can occur. Back pain alone is not necessarily sciatica. A healthcare professional should assess persistent or worsening symptoms.

Movement and physiotherapy

Once serious causes have been excluded, staying gently active, walking as tolerated and individually adapted exercises may help recovery. Prolonged bed rest is generally discouraged. The suitable plan depends on nerve findings, symptoms and other medical conditions.

When is emergency care needed?

Seek immediate emergency assessment for new bladder or bowel control changes, numbness around the genitals or bottom, or severe or worsening weakness or numbness in both legs. Severe symptoms after major trauma or with serious illness also need prompt medical review.

Questions about sciatica

Is rest always the best treatment?

No. Long periods in bed are usually discouraged for uncomplicated sciatica; suitable activity is generally encouraged.

Does every case require an operation?

No. Many people improve with conservative care, but severe or progressive neurological symptoms need urgent specialist assessment.
