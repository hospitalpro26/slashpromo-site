Neck Pain and Cervical Physiotherapy in Pano Aqil

Neck pain can have several causes. Learn when movement advice may help and when urgent assessment is needed.

What causes neck pain?

Muscle strain, desk posture, an awkward sleeping position, injuries and some nerve or joint problems can cause neck discomfort. Neck pain by itself does not prove cervical spondylosis. A clinician can assess symptoms when pain persists or worsens.

What rehabilitation may involve

For uncomplicated pain, an assessment may lead to gentle movement advice, an individualized exercise programme, ways to avoid prolonged fixed positions and suggestions for returning to ordinary activity. A cervical collar is not routinely recommended unless a clinician advises it for a particular problem.

When to get medical help

Get medical assessment for persistent or worsening pain, tingling or weakness in an arm, or symptoms after injury. Seek emergency care for new difficulty walking, loss of coordination, substantial limb weakness or sudden bladder or bowel control problems. Stroke-like symptoms require emergency attention.

Questions about neck pain

Does every patient with neck pain require an MRI?

No. Testing depends on the history, examination and warning signs; routine imaging is not necessary for every person with simple neck pain.

Is physiotherapy suitable for all neck injuries?

No. Significant injuries and neurological symptoms require medical assessment before rehabilitation.
