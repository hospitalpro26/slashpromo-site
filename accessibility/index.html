Accessibility

Accessibility information and feedback for SlashPromo visitors.

Effective: 5 November 2026 · Applies to the SlashPromo website.

Our commitment

We aim for an inclusive experience across mobile devices and desktop screens, including keyboard use, readable contrast, meaningful labels and respect for reduced-motion settings.

Design provisions

Navigation and interactive elements use semantic controls and visible focus indicators. Decorative animations are reduced for visitors who enable reduced motion preferences in their operating system.

Known limitations and feedback

We continue improving the site as it grows. If you encounter a barrier, please email info@slashcure.com and include the affected page, device or assistive technology, and the problem you encountered.
