Finding healthcare with SlashPromo

How can I find doctors in my city?

Enter a doctor name, city or specialty in the search above. You can also browse dedicated city pages for Sukkur, Karachi, Lahore, Khairpur, Shikarpur, Hyderabad and Pano Aqil.

Are OPD hours and appointments guaranteed?

No. SlashPromo displays publicly supplied profile information. Confirm working days, OPD timings, consultation fees and availability with the doctor or clinic before travelling.

How can I promote a hospital or clinic?

Use Promote Your Practice or contact the SlashPromo team to discuss promotional placements. Advertising and editorial content are identified separately.
