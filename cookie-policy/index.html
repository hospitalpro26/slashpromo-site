Cookie Policy

Information about cookies and similar technologies on SlashPromo.

Effective: 5 November 2026 · Applies to the SlashPromo website.

Our current use

The current version of SlashPromo does not intentionally set non-essential analytics or advertising cookies through its own site code. Search and filter interactions operate in your browser.

Optional location preference storage

If you explore several public pages, we may show an optional invitation to use location for nearby doctor listings. The page-visit counter and last visited public route are stored in browser storage for up to 24 hours, allowing navigation across tabs to count. If you accept location access, only the nearest listed city name is saved in your browser for up to 30 days, to prioritize relevant profiles. The browser-side code does not save or transmit your precise GPS coordinates. An unanswered invitation is deferred for one day, or for seven days if explicitly dismissed, to avoid repeated interruptions. These are browser storage entries, not advertising cookies. You may remove your preferred city beside directory search results or clear this site's storage in your browser. Location access remains optional and can be blocked or revoked in browser settings.

Infrastructure and other websites

Cloudflare and other infrastructure providers may use strictly necessary security or service cookies to protect and deliver the website. Linked third-party websites may have separate tracking technologies and privacy notices.

Future updates

If we introduce optional measurement, advertising trackers or consent-dependent cookies, we will update this policy and implement appropriate controls before activation.

Your choices

You may manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential security cookies can affect certain website functions.
